# Zipwit visual assets

`public/images/zipwit-logo.webp` is the transparent horizontal logo used in the
header and footer. It was edited with the built-in imagegen tool, then converted
to lossless WebP while preserving alpha. The white Z inside the violet mark
remains opaque. The Offers artwork and avatar are separate brand assets.

## Final logo edit prompt

Edit target: the supplied existing Zipwit horizontal logo. Task:
background-extraction ONLY. Remove the off-white/beige rectangular background to
genuine alpha transparency, including all spaces between and inside the letters.
Preserve the exact existing logo geometry, exact word 'zipwit', shapes,
proportions, alignment, violet #5b3df5, lime #c6f432, near-black #12121a lettering,
and white Z inside the violet icon. The white Z is intentional artwork and must
remain opaque white. Do not redesign, repaint, add shadow, add outline, add padding,
change font, alter letter shapes or crop. Same horizontal composition and tight
bounds. Transparent background, clean antialiased edges.
